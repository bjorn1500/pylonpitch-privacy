# Pylon Pitch – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 23 October 2026*

**Pylon Pitch** is a turn-based soccer game for Android, developed by **Fontaine Software**. This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

**In short:** you don't need an account, your progress is stored only on your phone, ads are shown through Unity LevelPlay (personalised only if you agree), and the "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play.

---

## 1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: the player name, profile picture and country flag you choose, your rank, coins, unlocked kits and other items, daily objectives, your settings, and your answer to the advertising question.

- This data is **not sent to us** or to anyone else. We do not run servers or user accounts.
- Uninstalling the game (or clearing its storage in Android Settings) deletes it.

---

## 2. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

Pylon Pitch is free and shows ads: an occasional full-screen ad between matches, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to double your coins). Ads are delivered by **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource Mobile Ltd., a Unity Technologies company) and its ad network partners, such as ironSource Ads and Unity Ads.

To show and measure ads, cap how often you see them and prevent fraud, these services may collect from your device:

- device identifiers, such as the **advertising ID** of your device;
- your IP address and the approximate location (country or region) derived from it;
- device and app information, such as phone model, operating system, language, and app version;
- information about the ads you see and tap.

**Your choice:** the first time you open the game we ask whether ads may be personalised. If you say no, the ad partners are told you did not consent (GDPR) and that your data may not be sold or shared (US state privacy laws), and you get non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in **Settings > Personalised ads**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

The ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

- Unity (including ironSource and LevelPlay): <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>
- Unity Ads: <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>

---

## 3. Purchases ("Remove Ads")

You can buy **Remove Ads** once to stop ads; rewards from rewarded ads can then be claimed without watching. The purchase is handled entirely by **Google Play**:

- we never see or store your payment details;
- the game only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own Remove Ads, and remembers that on your device;
- Google Play can restore the purchase on a new device signed in to the same Google account (Settings > Restore purchases).

Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

---

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
- We do not use our own analytics or tracking, and we do not sell personal data.
- There is no chat with other people and no user-generated content shared with others.

---

## 5. Children

Pylon Pitch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us and we will help.

---

## 6. Your rights

Because your game data stays on your device, you control it directly: uninstall the game or clear its storage to delete it. For data processed by the advertising partners, use the choices above or contact those partners via their policies. Under the GDPR and similar laws you may also contact us with any question or request about your data; we respond within 30 days.

---

## 7. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device. The advertising and Google Play services communicate over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
